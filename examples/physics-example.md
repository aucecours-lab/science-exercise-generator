# Physics Example

## Topic
Electric circuits

## Exercise
A lamp operates with a voltage of 6 V and a current of 0.5 A.

Calculate the electrical power.

## Correction
Formula:

P = U × I

P = 6 × 0.5

P = 3 W

The electrical power is 3 watts.

## Skills
- Apply a formula
- Calculate electrical power
- Use scientific units
