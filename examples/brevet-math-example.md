# Brevet Math Example

## Topic
Percentages and proportionality

## Exercise
A school has 240 students.
35% of the students are in year 9.

How many year 9 students are there?

## Correction
35% of 240 means:

240 × 35 / 100 = 84

There are 84 year 9 students.

## Skills
- Calculate a percentage
- Use proportional reasoning
- Write a clear numerical answer
